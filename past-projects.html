Cherry County

GH-5B, Tech Zone IV, Amrapali Dream Valley,
Greater Noida, UP 201306

Cherry County is a ready-to-move residential development spanning 12 acres in Tech Zone IV, Greater Noida West. The project comprises 12 towers rising up to 29 floors, with 1,750 apartments in 2, 3, and 4 BHK configurations ranging from 891 sq. ft. onwards. Residents enjoy a full suite of amenities including a clubhouse, swimming pool, gymnasium, and landscaped green spaces. The project is strategically located near Lotus Valley School, Yatharth Hospital, and Gaur City Mall, with direct access to the FNG Expressway and NH-24 — making it one of the best-connected addresses in Greater Noida West.

Cleo County

GH-05, Sector 121, Noida,
Uttar Pradesh 201301

One of Noida's most iconic residential landmarks, Cleo County is an Egyptian-themed township spread across 24.66 acres in Sector 121. The project features 24 towers, each rising to 28 floors, with a total of 2,638 luxury apartments in 3, 4, and 5 BHK configurations. Signature design elements include a Sphinx entrance, Giza-inspired courtyards, and resort-style landscaping. The project holds an IGBC Gold Certification for green building, incorporating rainwater harvesting, solar lighting, and cross-ventilation design. Located on a 70-metre-wide road, it offers direct connectivity to Delhi, Noida Expressway, and the proposed Sector 121 Metro Station.

Orange County

Ahinsa Khand 1, Indirapuram,
Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh

A landmark Mediterranean-inspired gated community in Ahinsa Khand 1, Indirapuram — one of NCR's most sought-after residential neighbourhoods. Completed in 2011, Orange County spans 10 acres and comprises 16 towers of G+18 floors with 896 apartments in 2, 3, and 4 BHK configurations ranging from 1,224 to 4,455 sq. ft. The development is known for its striking orange-hued exteriors, 80% green cover, and world-class amenities including a clubhouse, pool, gym, and children's play area. Located minutes from the Delhi border and Vaishali Metro Station, with easy access to NH-24.

Olive County

GH-9, Sector 5, Vasundhara,
Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh 201012

A ready-to-move residential community spread across 13 acres in Sector 5, Vasundhara — just 10 minutes from the Delhi border. Completed in 2011, Olive County comprises 16 towers of 14 floors each, with 868 apartments in 2 and 3 BHK configurations from 1,413 sq. ft. The project has been recognised as a Best Eco-Friendly Sustainable Project, featuring natural ventilation design, EV charging stations, and eco-friendly roofing. Amenities include a swimming pool, gymnasium, jogging track, clubhouse, and 24/7 security. Located close to DiViNiTi School, Mohan Nagar Metro Station, and major business parks.
