MKS County

Sector 2, Opposite K.R. Mangalam University,
Sohna, Haryana 122103

MKS County is a residential plotted development located in Sector 2, Sohna — one of the fastest-growing corridors in the Delhi NCR region. Positioned directly opposite K.R. Mangalam University, the project sits at the heart of an emerging educational and residential belt in Haryana. Sohna offers excellent connectivity to Gurugram via NH-248A and to Delhi via the Southern Peripheral Road. With rapid infrastructure development, growing institutional presence, and strong long-term appreciation potential, MKS County offers investors and homebuyers a rare opportunity to own land in a corridor that is transforming rapidly.

MKS Shiva Market

Plot No. 1, Multilevel Car Parking, Shiva Market,
Pitampura, Delhi 110034

MKS Shiva Market is a premium commercial development in the heart of Pitampura, one of West Delhi's most established and high-footfall retail and business destinations. Located adjacent to the Shiva Market multilevel car parking facility, the project benefits from exceptional visibility and daily footfall from the surrounding residential catchment. Pitampura is well-served by the Delhi Metro (Kohat Enclave and Pitampura stations on the Red Line), ensuring strong accessibility for businesses and their customers. MKS Shiva Market represents MKS Ventures' foray into Delhi's commercial real estate market — bringing the same commitment to quality and long-term value that has defined the firm's residential portfolio.
